\# 🌿 GreenBloom - Plant Nursery Management System



GreenBloom is a web-based Plant Nursery Management System developed using

Python, Streamlit and MySQL.



The system helps a plant nursery manage plants, suppliers, customers,

billing and sales reports through a simple and interactive dashboard.



\---



\## 🌱 Project Overview



GreenBloom provides a centralized system for managing daily nursery

operations.



The application allows users to:



\- Manage plant inventory

\- Add, update and delete plants

\- Upload plant images

\- Manage suppliers

\- Manage customers

\- Generate customer bills

\- Automatically update plant stock after sales

\- Track customer purchase history

\- Generate sales and inventory reports

\- Export reports as CSV files



\---
